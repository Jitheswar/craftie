Speed boost (optional): TypeSafe Jev

Jev answers simple orders like "come here" or "get 10 oak logs" in a fraction of a second, so Craftie skips the slower AI for those. Get a key at typesafe.ai.

3Where should Craftie play?

  1. Open your world in Minecraft.
  2. Press Esc → Open to LAN. With the Custom LAN mod, set Online Mode: Off (needed for offline accounts). The Craftie mod (step 4) does this step and the next one for you.
  3. Click Start LAN World. Craftie finds it and joins by itself.

Works with offline-mode servers and friends' worlds. Craftie joins as an extra player.

Only you can give Craftie orders. Anyone else's messages are ignored.

4Add the Craftie mod to your game (optional)

Opens your worlds to LAN by itself, so Craftie joins with no clicks. On Minecraft 26 and newer it also shows what Craftie is doing on screen, adds an order key (G) and /craftie, and gives Craftie its skin. Only you need it; friends install nothing.
